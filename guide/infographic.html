01 — The Core Loop
Every day you allocate up to 24 hours. Most days just resolve stats. Every 7th day is also a match. Every 39th round closes a season and feeds the league ladder. Match winnings fund the Shop, which raises your ceilings and multipliers. End Day ▶ plays one day; To Match ▶▶ repeats the same plan through the next match (End Week ▶▶ outside the season, up to 7 days) and shows a week summary — stopping early to let you re-plan on an injury, losing/finding a job or going pro, burnout, a new technique, or Nutrition shrinking the day below your plan.

04 — The 7 Case Specialties
Only 1–3 skills are "active" each week, revealed only once the previous week ends. Preseason is the exception — every skill is trainable. Any skill under 1h/day (active-but-neglected, or simply locked this week) rusts — unless its Coach is hired, which stops it rusting entirely.

🎓 Experience — XP 0–100 per skill (new in v4.75)
Earned by playing, not training: every match a skill is in focus earns it XP, win or lose. Only while you play in League 4 or higher (relegation to League 5 pauses it). Never rusts — and it's a floor: a skill never rusts below its XP, and a ⭐ specialisation you swap out only drops to its XP if that's above the lower cap (XP 100 → it stays at 100). Shown as the violet strip along the bottom of each skill bar.

05 — The Four Vitals
Gym, Sleep, Relax and Food each have their own decay threshold, and each feeds a different multiplier elsewhere in the system.
🏃 Gym → Physical Health
Cap: 70 base, +10 per Sports Physio level (max 100)
gain = effectiveHours × 0.6 × physio × rest × diminish
Rest below 80 wears it down: max(0, 80−rest)×0.04/day regardless of Gym hours. Below 1h/day: detrain −0.35/day (Recovery Program reduces).
Injury risk: every Gym hour adds 2.5% that day — 0h is the only risk-free day, 8h (the max) is 20% (Physio cuts it 20/35/50%). Injury: −5 to −10 Health, locks the Gym 3–5 days (Recovery shortens). Never stops you playing a match.
🌙 Sleep → Rest
Threshold: 7h/day ideal · Cap: 100
below 7h: −(7−h)×1.3×sleepApp   above: +min(h−7,3)×1.4
The only vital with a training-effectiveness effect (skills and Gym) — a smooth slide, no steps:
multiplier = 1 + clamp((rest − 70) / 30, 0, 1)

🥗 Food → Nutrition
Threshold: 1h/day · Cap: 100 · Starts at 75
gain = effectiveHours × 5 × diminish × nutritionist below 1h: −9/day flat (Nutritionist reduces)
Doesn't touch training effectiveness at all — its only job is Section 02's daily hours budget. Rest already owns training effectiveness; this is deliberately a separate lever.

End Week / To Match carry on with your plan as it is — unassigned extra hours go unused (the summary says how many).
06 — Work, Pro Duties & Chances
Work funds everything else — 9h/day, except on paid 🏖️ annual leave — the whole 21-day preseason, which starts the day after the Final (Work / Pro Duties greyed out, full pay, no chances at risk and used ones still come back, a Pro’s technique clock paused; a job search carries on as normal). Up to 4h of overtime on top pays half the job’s hourly rate (pay ÷ 9 ÷ 2 — $4/h at the starting $70/day) per extra hour. You start each job with 3.0 chances; the job-security bar shows exactly what today's shortfall will cost before you commit to it. Pay grows with tenure and a flat cost of living runs in the background — stay employed and you profit, lose your job and the bills don't stop (and your pay rate starts over).

Pro technique queue
There are 20 techniques. Every 38 days as a pro (Half 19, Short 10), one you haven't mastered queues up needing a random 30–45h (Half 15–23, Short 8–12) — about 2.5 years as a pro to master all 20 in every length, after which no more arrive. Masteries are kept for good. Any Pro Duties hours beyond the 5h minimum go toward the front of the queue (FIFO — one at a time; spare hours roll on to the next). Falling behind never costs progress, it just queues — but every not-yet-mastered technique costs 5% match performance, stacking (capped at 100%).
Chances recover automatically: each chance lost expires 21 days (3 weeks) after it's earned.
💸 Cost of living, tenure & the year-end summary
$50/day cost of living, charged every single day no matter your employment status — preseason, regular season, playoffs, offseason, employed, Pro, or unemployed, it never stops. Pay is tied to still having the role, not to hitting the exact hour target that day — falling short costs a chance (see the tables below), not income. It isn't flat either — it's tenure-based:

07 — Match Day
One match every 7 days in the regular season (plus playoffs). Both sides get a match-day rating = performance × 8 + focus-skill edge + luck, and the higher one wins — unless the scores end up level: a ⏱ dead heat (real totals within 60 — ≈1 even game in 4), won by the faster finish. No draws, league or playoffs. Rating isn't part of it — it moves with results as a record. The luck is built so the chance of winning is exactly the win probability shown in the topbar preview — upsets always stay possible.

Match-day rating & luck
matchDay = perf × 8 + edge + luck luck = Gumbel(scale 250/ln10, centred 0) — one per side P(win) = P(gap > 60 after luck) + P(within 60 → shown level) × P(you finish faster)
The result is decided by the real luck draws (so upsets happen exactly as often as the win chance says). Rivals' skills are anchored to their league: ⭐ skills 10 under the league cap, ±0.06 per rating point from the league's middle. The luck shown is cosmetic: each side's real draw at ⅕ of its size (median ≈ 24), topped up just enough to explain an upset, so the columns always add up; and performance uses all 7 skills averaged (55%) for you and rivals (rivals: Health/Calm/Rest at 80); the week's focus skills count in the edge. 🎯 Focus-skill edge: whoever averages higher in the week's focus skills gets 10 match-day rating per point ahead. League tables rank by wins, then head-to-head among tied players, then rating, and show W–L and form (last 5). Seasons from before v4.76 keep their draws (W–D–L).

08 — Season Structure
Three game lengths, picked when a game starts, each with its own save slot (☰ → 🎮 Games): Short — 10 per league (9 rounds), top 4 playoffs, 2 up/2 down, 1-week preseason; Half — 20 (19), top 8, 3/3, 2 weeks; Full — 40 (39), top 16, 4/4, 3 weeks. Same 5 leagues in all; camp is a week per playoff round. The day of the Final is the year’s last day for everyone. Reach the Final, win or lose, and the new year starts straight after it. Miss the playoffs (28 days) or get knocked out (R16 21, QF 14, SF 7) and you go to training camp until then, which gives back as training the time the rest of the playoffs would have taken — so an early exit never costs you prep time.

5 tiers, 40 competitors each — 199 persistent named rivals in total, spanning all 5 leagues, plus your own slot. Every tier runs a genuine round-robin in lockstep with your own matches, so all 5 tables are live from round 1. New careers start at League 5.

10 — Staff & Items
13 staff — 7 skill Coaches (5 levels) and 6 support staff (3 levels). Staff are hired one week at a time: the week's wage is paid up front (pro-rated × days left ÷ 7 if hired mid-week; moving a hired one up a level costs just the extra wages), a week ends after each match, and nothing renews by default — you choose again every week. Every coach and support-staff member has an optional 🔁 Auto-rehire switch (a coach is kept on even when their skill isn’t in that week’s focus): when a week ends they’re kept on at the same level for the next one (at its pro-rated wage) if the cash is there; if not, they’re off that week, the switch turns itself off, and the log and a “Couldn’t rehire staff” screen say so. They only work while hired. Each higher level is a one-off fee that unlocks only once your peak league allows it, and costs more per week. Wages are tuned so spare weekly money covers ~90% of what you'd want in a 1-skill week and ~40% in a 3-skill week.
